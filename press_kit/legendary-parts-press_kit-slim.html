01 / Who we are

Parts first, with a workshop behind them.

Legendary Parts is the parts business — new and second-hand Harley-Davidson OEM and aftermarket parts. We stock deep, including older aftermarket parts that are hard to find anywhere else, and ship reliably across Europe, with strong delivery into the UK, Germany, Italy and Spain as well as France.

Garage On The Road is the sister workshop on the same site — real mechanics doing builds, restorations and used-bike sales. It means the parts advice comes from the bench, not a spreadsheet: fitment, compatibility and what actually works.

The physical store doubles as a community hub, hosting biker meet-ups and events — walls of memorabilia, a warm welcome, and people who'll talk fitment with a seasoned mechanic or a first-time owner alike.
